# Digital Microloan Onboarding Optimization (Business Analysis Case Study) 
## 1. Executive Summary 
A mid-tier South African digital lender is experiencing a **42% customer drop-off rate** during mobile microloan applications. Manual FICA document uploads and delayed affordability assessments cause turnaround times to exceed 48 hours. This project defines the business requirements, process redesign, and Agile backlog required to automate KYC verification and cut loan approval times to under 15 minutes.
## 2. Business Objectives & Success KPIs 
* **Reduce Application Drop-Off:** Decrease funnel abandonment from 42% to **under 18%** within 6 months of launch.
* **Accelerate Turnaround Time (TAT):** Cut average loan decisioning from 48 hours to **under 15 minutes**.
* **Ensure Regulatory Compliance:** Maintain 100% adherence to South African **FICA**, **POPIA**, and **National Credit Act (NCA)** affordability rules.
